The Telemetry Equation

Net Gas (W_net) = Total Gross Weight (W_gross) − Tare Weight (15.3 kg)

Frequently Asked Questions & Technical Specs

Every LPG cylinder manufactured in India is legally stamped with its exact empty vessel tare weight (typically between 15.1 kg and 15.8 kg). During initial setup, FuelSense's calibration engine records this value. The device continuously subtracts the vessel tare weight from the total gross measurement, yielding pure combustible gas mass with sub-gram resolution.
